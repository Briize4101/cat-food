Uniam's "Three Features"

Uniam specializes in cats, so
we are dedicated to the happiness of cats.

Cat-first as we specialize in cats

We focus solely on cats, and together with a team of experts, including cat-loving veterinarians, we develop products that are tailored to the natural physical characteristics of cats. We breathe in cats and spit out ideas. This cat-first brand was born from that team.

Synergy between medicine and creativity

With "cat health" at its core, veterinarians are at the forefront of product development based on medical evidence, but they also keep a playful spirit. We want Kainishi to "have fun while learning more about cats," so we value the combination of medicine and creativity.

Uniam quality created by cat lovers

Uniam's greatest resource is its deep love for cats. The happiness of cats is our number one priority, and we turn our ideas into reality through repeated prototyping and rigorous testing with cats. As a team made up entirely of cat-crazed people, we spare no time or effort for cats.

Characteristics of Fresh Food
Uniam's expert team
Uniam develops its services together with cat-loving professionals in various fields.
Recipe Supervision

Susan Lauten

doctor

She holds a PhD in Animal Nutrition and Biomedical Science from Auburn University in Alabama, USA. He has overseen numerous recipes in the United States, a leading country in fresh food. For over 20 years, he has served as the representative of Pet Nutrition Consulting, providing nutritional advice and recipes to veterinarians and pet food companies. His areas of expertise are diverse, ranging from complete nutritional foods to therapeutic diets for pets with medical problems and diet prescriptions.

Recipe Supervision

Rina Igarashi

veterinarian

She is a certified veterinary dermatologist by the Japanese Board of Veterinary Dermatology. He majored in veterinary nutritional therapy at Chi University in the US. After graduating from Nihon University's School of Veterinary Medicine, he worked in several animal hospitals and also engaged in secondary care as a dermatologist at the Tokyo University of Agriculture and Technology Animal Medical Center. He is active globally, having gained clinical experience at the Hong Kong Veterinary University Hospital under the supervision of a specialist from the European Board of Dermatology. His favorite part of cats is their slightly visible front teeth.

Personalization Supervision

Sawako Takeda

veterinarian

Graduated from Nihon University School of Veterinary Medicine. After completing his training at the University of Tokyo Animal Medical Center, he worked at a veterinary hospital in Kanagawa Prefecture. He is currently studying hard in the United States with his rescue cat Jewel. He is such a big cat lover that he dreamed of becoming a "cat doctor" from an early age and became a veterinarian. He believes that he was able to talk to cats after obtaining his veterinary license. His favorite part of a cat is the line from the forehead to the ears.
